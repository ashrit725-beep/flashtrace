FlashTrace demo

Read this before you continue

This local server hosts a fictional sports page, “Courtside Live”, built as a test subject for FlashTrace.

Warning

The page behind the link below flashes. It contains a loader that strobes between white and black five times a second, and a badge that flashes red four times a second. Both are there deliberately so FlashTrace has something hazardous to find.

Do not open it if you have photosensitive epilepsy, or if flashing light has ever triggered a seizure, a migraine or discomfort for you. Do not open it on a large screen in a dark room, and do not show it to anyone without warning them first.

You do not need to look at it

The audit runs in a headless browser. No window opens and nothing flashes on your screen. With this server running, open a terminal and run:

flashtrace audit http://127.0.0.1:8765/site/

FlashTrace captures frames, measures luminance and red changes, applies the WCAG 2.3.1 thresholds and writes a report with still-frame evidence only. The report itself never flashes.

What the page contains

#loader420 × 300 px, white/black, 5 per secondFlagged: large area, large luminance change, more than three flashes per second
#live-badge96 × 32 px, red/grey, 4 per secondNot flagged: about 3% of a 341 × 256 px window, under the 25% area rule
#next-upSlow opacity fade, once every 2 sNot flagged: half a flash per second

If you do open it

  • The header has a “Pause all motion” button. It stops every animation on the page at once; press it again to resume.
  • If your operating system’s reduced-motion setting is on, the page shows no animation at all and says so.
  • Close the tab when you are done. The page does not reload or redirect itself.

The hazardous page

Open the flashing test page (/site/) — flashes at 4–5 Hz

Opens in this tab. Served from this computer only (127.0.0.1); nothing is sent anywhere.

Analysis aid, not a compliance certification.

A PASS from FlashTrace means “no WCAG 2.3.1 violation detected under the tested conditions”. It never certifies a page as compliant.
